# Code of conduct

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and safe.
Contributors must communicate professionally, welcome good-faith questions,
respect privacy, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or the
publication of another person's credentials or private data.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior puts people or credentials at risk. Report conduct concerns privately
through the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not use public issues for sensitive
reports.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, and the safety of affected
people. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is not acceptable.
